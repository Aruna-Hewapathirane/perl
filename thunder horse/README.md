### 🎮 Game Overview
You control a spaceship at the bottom of the screen, navigating a scrolling starfield. Your goal is to shoot down incoming waves of enemy ships, advance through levels, and ultimately defeat a massive boss.

### 🕹️ Controls
* **Left Arrow / Right Arrow:** Move your spaceship left and right.
* **Up Arrow / Down Arrow:** Move your spaceship up and down.
* **Spacebar:** Fire lasers to destroy enemies.
* **Any Key (At Start/Game Over):** Starts the game from the title screen or restarts it after you lose.

### 🚀 Gameplay & Progression
* **Levels:** The game features up to **5 levels**, each indicated by a unique enemy color scheme:
  * **Level 1:** White
  * **Level 2:** Green
  * **Level 3:** Magenta
  * **Level 4:** Light Purple
  * **Level 5:** Red
* **The Boss Fight:** In the final stretch, a massive boss ship named **Thunderhorse** will spawn at the top of the screen. It has a large health pool (**50 HP**) and moves side-to-side across the screen.
* **Particles & Sound:** Destroying enemies triggers visual particle explosions and plays sound effects (using the Linux `aplay` command utility).

### 💀 Game Over
If an enemy ship or the boss collides with your spaceship, it is **Game Over**. You can press any key to reset your score to 0, return to Level 1, and try again.
